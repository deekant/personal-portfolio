Design and Webflow build for the Candela Search site redesign.

Approach

I'll work from your approved prototype page by page in Figma, keeping the structure and content you've signed off and turning it into one coherent system: a consistent icon set, the Candela arcs as a recurring motif, a proper type scale, and a component library shared across every page and the NEXT by Candela variant.

Where the prototype can go further structurally, I'll flag it rather than silently change it — it's the starting point, not a limit.

Motion is built natively in Webflow with GSAP, so what you see in the handoff is the real thing, not a Figma approximation. The four signature moments (rings, stat counters, logo strip, timeline) get prototyped early enough to catch reduced‑motion and keyboard issues before they're repeated across templates.

I design and build myself — no handover gap between the person who designed a component and the person wiring it up.
