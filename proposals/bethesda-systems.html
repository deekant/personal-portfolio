Approach

Hi Jennifer, here is how I would design and build the new Bethesda Systems website.

  1. /01

    I design and build myself, in Figma and Webflow. The page you approve is the page I build, so nothing gets lost in a handover.

  2. /02

    Webflow, set up in your name. You own the domain, design, content and all assets, with full admin access to the CMS, Search Console, Analytics and the page source. Webflow can export the code, so you can move providers at any time.

  3. /03

    Custom design and custom copy, written around the questions your buyers ask: what you install, for whom, where, on which platforms, and what proves it. No themes or template copy.

  4. /04

    Your marketing team publishes projects, guides and FAQs in the Webflow Editor from ready templates, without breaking the design.

AI and
Search

Built to be read, cited and recommended.

  1. /01

    Every page is server-rendered, so its full text is in the page source. Nothing important sits behind scripts or clicks.

  2. /02

    Clean URLs, canonical tags, an XML sitemap submitted to Search Console, and no accidental noindex or snippet blocks.

  3. /03

    Structured data on every relevant page: LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, Review and Breadcrumb.

  4. /04

    One consistent company description and address, 4840 Cordell Ave, Bethesda, MD 20814, on every page, written so you are never confused with the medical Bethesda System.

  5. /05

    A few strong service-area pages for Bethesda, Potomac, Chevy Chase, DC and Northern Virginia, not many near-identical city pages.

What’s
Included

Every page in your brief, plus the integrations.

  1. /01

    Pages: homepage, 8 service pages, platform pages for Control4, Savant, Lutron and other key brands, builders, architects and designers with a partner inquiry form, service areas, about and team, service plans, showroom, and contact.

  2. /02

    CMS templates: Projects with location, scope, systems, results, before and after photos and video with transcript. Guides and FAQs titled as buyer questions, with FAQ schema.

  3. /03

    Zoho CRM: the consultation and partner forms send leads to Zoho with every field mapped, including name, address, project type and systems of interest. I test every field end to end before launch.

  4. /04

    Performance and accessibility: mobile-first, optimized photos and video, strong Core Web Vitals and WCAG 2.1 AA.

  5. /05

    Copy, photography and video for the new pages.

  6. /06

    Migration: the pages and posts worth keeping, with a 301 redirect from every old URL.

  7. /07

    Training for your team, recorded, plus a short how-to guide.
