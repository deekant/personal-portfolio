FAQs

Got questions? I’ve got answers.
Here’s everything you need to know about working with me.
/01
How do you approach a new project?
I don’t start with visuals. I start with the problem.
We define your goals, audience, and business priorities first. Only then do I design a solution that actually converts, not just looks nice.
/02
How long does a project usually take?
Most projects take 4–8 weeks, depending on complexity.
You’ll get a clear timeline before we start — no vague promises, no endless delays.
/03
What happens after launch?
Your site doesn’t die after publishing.
I offer ongoing support for updates, improvements, and performance tweaks so it keeps working for your business.
/04
Will my site be mobile-optimized?
Of course. Most users are on mobile.
If your site doesn’t work there, it’s already failing.
/05
Do you work with other teams or agencies?
Yes.
I can integrate into existing teams or handle the full design + development process solo.
/06
Can I edit the site myself later?
Yes.
You’ll get a CMS setup that’s easy to manage without breaking the design abd all needed manuals and support to maintain a website yourself.
